Fashion Boutique — Checkout Friction Removal

A Shopify fashion store was driving solid traffic from Meta ads but converting at under 1.2%. The revenue was there — it just wasn't making it to checkout.

The Problem

Their checkout had 6 steps, no trust badges, a broken mobile cart experience, and product pages with no urgency signals. Visitors were bouncing at the add-to-cart stage.

What We Did

  • Rebuilt the checkout to 2-step with guest checkout enabled
  • Added trust badges, secure payment icons and return policy
  • Fixed the mobile cart drawer and sticky CTA
  • Rewrote product page copy with scarcity and social proof
Result: Conversion rate went from 1.2% to 1.66% in 30 days. Revenue hit £14k — their first £10k month ever.
Shopify / Health & Wellness
3.1× ROAS £22k in 6 weeks

Supplements Brand — Full Funnel Rebuild

A health supplements brand had good products and a decent following but their ad spend was eating their margin. They were spending £4k/month to make £5k.

The Problem

Landing pages were sending paid traffic to the homepage. No dedicated landing pages, no email capture, and no retargeting sequence. Ad spend was wasted on cold traffic with nowhere to convert.

What We Did

  • Built dedicated landing pages per ad set
  • Set up a 5-email welcome and abandoned cart sequence
  • Created a retargeting audience from existing purchasers
  • Restructured Google Shopping campaigns by margin tier
Result: ROAS improved from 1.25× to 3.1× within 6 weeks. Monthly revenue crossed £22k — up from £5k.
Wix / Home & Living
52% more leads £11k first month

Home Décor Store — Site Redesign + SEO

A home décor brand was relying entirely on Instagram for traffic. Their Wix site looked outdated, loaded slowly and had no SEO presence whatsoever.

The Problem

The site had a 4.8 second load time, no product schema, no collection page structure and zero Google visibility. 100% of traffic was social — and if the algorithm shifted, so did their revenue.

What We Did

  • Redesigned the site with a clean, fast-loading layout
  • Added schema markup to all product and category pages
  • Built out 8 SEO-optimised collection pages
  • Reduced load time from 4.8s to 1.6s
Result: Organic traffic increased 52% in 60 days. First Google-driven sale week unlocked £11k in orders.
